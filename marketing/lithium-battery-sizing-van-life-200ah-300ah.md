How to choose between 200Ah and 300Ah lithium for full-time van life? It comes down to one number: your daily Ah budget.

Here's what real van lifers actually consume:

🔋 Minimalist couple (summer): ~44 Ah/day → 200Ah = 3.6 days autonomy

Our sizing formula:
Battery Ah = (daily Ah × days of autonomy) ÷ 0.8

Example: 90 Ah/day × 2 days ÷ 0.8 = 225Ah → go 300Ah.
