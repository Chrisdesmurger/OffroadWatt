About OffroadWatt

Independent project Updated Jul 5, 2026

OffroadWatt is a free, independent tool that helps you size and understand the electrical system of a campervan, motorhome, caravan or van — before you spend a euro on batteries, panels or a charger.

What it does

You enter your appliances (with their power and daily hours), your battery bank (AGM, GEL or lithium, 12 V or 24 V), your solar panels and your alternator charging. OffroadWatt then calculates, in real time, your daily consumption, your solar and alternator production, the residual deficit and your autonomy in days — all expressed in amp-hours (Ah), the way leisure batteries are actually sold in Europe.

How the numbers are calculated

The engine is deliberately transparent — the same arithmetic any installer would do by hand:

  • Consumption: each appliance's watts × hours/day = watt-hours, summed, then divided by the pack voltage to give Ah/day.
  • Battery: usable capacity = rated Ah × depth of discharge (50 % for lead-acid AGM/GEL, 80 % for LiFePO4).
  • Solar: panel watts × peak-sun-hours for your region × MPPT efficiency, converted to Ah/day.
  • Alternator: dedicated amps × driving hours × a 0.7 cable/regulator efficiency factor.

The data behind it

OffroadWatt ships with 33 real battery models (AGM, GEL and lithium, 12 V and 24 V), 41 geographic sun zones worldwide, and a community appliance catalogue that grows as people search for real components and their measured consumption. The blog documents the methodology in depth, with worked examples and comparison tables.

Why it's brand-neutral

OffroadWatt has no battery, panel or charger to sell. Recommendations are driven by your numbers, not by an affiliate quota — the opposite of a manufacturer's sizing wizard. It's built and maintained by a solo van-electrics enthusiast, and the source is public on GitHub.

Languages

The calculator and the blog are available in English, French and Spanish, with region-aware vocabulary (campervan / fourgon / autocaravana) and solar production tuned to your part of the world.

Who writes these guides

The blog is published under the byline Camille Aubert — the editorial identity of OffroadWatt. It's a disclosed pen name, not a separate person: every guide is researched, written and reviewed by the same solo van-electrics enthusiast who builds the calculator, using the transparent method described above and cross-checked against real product datasheets. Corrections are welcome via the public GitHub repository.
